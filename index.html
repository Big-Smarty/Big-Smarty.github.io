BigSmarty Tech Publisher

BigSmarty Tech Publisher is the private publishing tool behind the YouTube channel @bigsmartytech, which makes technical tutorials, explainers and AI and tech news videos.

What the app does

  • Uploads finished videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel and sets their title, description, thumbnail, category and visibility.
  • Adds those videos to the channel's own playlists and uploads their subtitle tracks (English, German and Simplified Chinese).
  • Reads the videos back after upload to check that everything arrived as intended.
  • Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (read-only) so the channel can see which videos people actually watch.

Who can use it

Nobody else. The app runs on the channel owner's own computer and only ever signs in to the channel owner's Google account. There is no sign-up, no public login and no access to anyone else's data.

How the app handles Google user data is described in the Privacy Policy. Use of the app is covered by the Terms of Service.
